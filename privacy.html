Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026.

What we store

Your quiz progress, flashcard ratings, and study preferences are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). This data never leaves your device: PassTheFE has no user accounts and our servers never receive it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, approximate location, device type). Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads that keep PassTheFE free. Google and its advertising partners use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the web.

You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info/choices. See also Google's advertising privacy information.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed only by Google (Analytics, AdSense) under Google's terms.

Children

PassTheFE is intended for adult FE exam candidates, not children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version here with a new "last updated" date.
